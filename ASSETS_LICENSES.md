# Asset licences — Masjid al-Haram 3D Explorer (`/haram`)

## Summary

The 3D model and almost every texture are generated in code by this project (see
`haram-src/src/world/`). The exceptions are **photographs of the real kiswah** used for its
belt (hizam) and door curtain (sitara), and the **plan of the mosque and the buildings around
it**, derived from OpenStreetMap data — both listed below with their licences — and one
font, **Vazirmatn**, for Arabic-script text (also used by the main pages). No third-party 3D
models or audio are used. The only external code shipped to visitors is three.js.

## External assets that were evaluated and not used

The brief asked to prefer an existing, legally reusable model of Masjid al-Haram. These were
found and assessed (October 2026):

| Asset | Creator | Source | Licence | Why it was not used |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| "Masjid Al Haram" | farhad.Guli | [Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/c3b26f8c1d424daf9343c1b7d837b32d) | CC BY 4.0 (as declared by the uploader) | Downloads require a Sketchfab account (the download API refused unauthenticated access), so it could not be obtained here; provenance of the uploaded model could not be verified. |
| "Masjid al-Haram (Great Mosque of Mecca)" | alfinann | [Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/329bb58e51c94ea28382053a8d11d8d9) | CC BY 4.0 (as declared by the uploader) | ~465,000 faces — too heavy for phones; account required to download. |
| Several "Kaaba" models (e.g. by agrees_putra, Cubic_Studio, menarzuw) | various | Sketchfab | CC BY 4.0 (as declared) | Account required; unverified provenance; inconsistent proportions. |
| "Shrine" | Aidan K McLaughlin | [Poly Pizza](https://poly.pizza/m/a68qNnAC4m-) | CC BY 3.0 | Stylised low-poly object; not a faithful or respectful representation for this purpose. |

Why generated geometry was chosen instead:

- **Licensing is certain.** "CC BY" on a user-upload site is the uploader's declaration; whether
  the uploader had the right to grant it cannot be checked. Generated geometry has no such
  question.
- **Accuracy can be controlled.** The Kaaba and the features around it are built from the
  published approximate dimensions in `SOURCES.md`, instead of inheriting someone else's
  unknown approximations.
- **Performance.** The whole environment is about 100,000 triangles and needs no model download
  at all.
- **Harder to lift.** There is no ready-made model file to download (see `HARAM.md`, "Security
  and copy protection").

## Adding a third-party asset later

1. Confirm the licence on the creator's own page, and that commercial/website use is allowed
   (CC0, CC BY, or similar). "Free to download" is not a licence.
2. Add a row to the table above: name, creator, source URL, licence, required attribution,
   restrictions, and where it is used.
3. If attribution is required, also credit it in the explorer's Help panel
   (`help.creditsBody` in `haram-src/src/i18n/strings.ts`). Never hide required attribution.
4. ShareAlike (CC BY-SA) assets require derived versions of that asset to carry the same
   licence; avoid them for anything you also want to protect.
