«قوفڵی بەرنامەکان» و خزمەتگوزاری دەستپێگەیشتن (Accessibility)

تایبەتمەندیی «قوفڵی بەرنامەکان» ڕاژەی AccessibilityService ی ئەندرۆید بەکاردەهێنێت تەنیا بۆ ئەوەی بزانێت کام بەرنامە هاتە پێشەوەی شاشەکە، تاکو شاشەی قوفڵ بەسەر ئەو بەرنامانەدا پیشان بدات کە خۆت هەڵتبژاردوون. ناوەڕۆکی شاشەکە ناخوێنێتەوە. ئەوەی دەینووسیت و وشەی نهێنی و نامەکانیشت نابینێت. هیچ لەمانە لە مۆبایلەکەت دەرناچێت. ئەم ڕاژەیە تەنیا دوای ڕەزامەندیی خۆت لە ئەپەکەدا هەڵدەکرێت و هەر کاتێک بتەوێت لە ڕێکخستنەکانی مۆبایلەکەت دەیکوژێنیتەوە. مۆڵەتی «نیشاندان لەسەر بەرنامەکانی تر» تەنیا بۆ پیشاندانی شاشەی قوفڵە.

About this policy

This page explains what information the app “Qurani Piroz — Pertuki Xwda” (com.alandkawaali.qurani_piroz_partuki_xwda) uses, what leaves your phone, and why. Developer: Aland Kawa Ali. Contact: alandkawaali@gmail.com.

The app has no user accounts and no ads. We do not sell any information or use it for advertising.

What stays on your phone

  • Settings, bookmarks, favourite verses, reading position, and the apps you choose in App Lock.
  • Camera (“Hana”): frames are analysed on the phone and not stored. A photo leaves the phone only if you choose to publish it to the map (see below).
  • Microphone (recitation checker): normally your voice is handled only by the phone’s own on-device speech recogniser, and no audio is recorded. If that is not available, you can choose server mode: your recitation audio then goes to the phone’s speech service (Google’s on Android, Apple’s on iPhone). The app asks before turning it on and shows a notice while it is on.
  • Location for prayer times, Qibla, sun and moon is calculated on the phone.

App Lock and the Accessibility Service

The App Lock feature uses Android’s AccessibilityService API only to detect which app comes to the front of the screen, so it can show a lock screen over the apps you chose to lock. It does not read screen content, what you type, passwords or messages, and nothing it sees leaves your phone. The service is turned on only after you agree to an in-app disclosure, and you can turn it off at any time in your phone’s settings. The “display over other apps” permission is used only to show that lock screen.

What leaves your phone, and why

  • A random device ID: the app creates a random number (not your phone’s hardware ID). It is sent with comments, multiplayer games, App Lock duels, map photo submissions and the live user count, so these features work and a device that abuses them can be blocked.
  • A name you type (optional): shown with your comment, in games, or with a photo you send to the map.
  • Comments and descriptions: what you write is stored and shown to other users. A filter blocks offensive words, and comments can be reported.
  • AI questions: your question and the verse it is about are sent through our server to AI providers (Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter) to generate the answer. Questions are not linked to your name or identity. AI answers may be wrong.
  • Map photos and location (optional): if you choose to send a “Hana” photo to the map, the photo and its precise location are stored. Each one is reviewed by hand and appears on the map only if approved.
  • Location: for weather (“Kashnama”) your approximate location (about 1 km) is sent to Open-Meteo, and to show place names it is sent to OpenStreetMap (Nominatim). Map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap.
  • Notifications: OneSignal delivers notifications. It receives a notification ID and basic device information (such as OS type and language).
  • Quran keyboard: if you use the AI search in the app’s Quran keyboard, the question in its search box is sent through our server to the AI providers when you tap search. The question may be pre-filled from the text you were typing, but never from password fields. The keyboard does not read or send anything else you type.
  • Reports: if you report an AI answer or a comment, that answer or comment, the reason, your note (if any) and your random device ID are sent to us so we can review it.
  • Content downloads: Quran recitations and Hevi’s data are downloaded from our servers and Quran.com. Like any website, these servers see standard technical information such as your IP address.

The version from our website (not Google Play) also offers optional location-based reminders: if you turn them on, your location is used in the background and nearby places are looked up on OpenStreetMap (Overpass). The Google Play version does not include this feature.

Who receives information

Supabase (database, storage and our AI relay), OneSignal, AI providers (Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter), Open-Meteo, OpenStreetMap, and — only in recitation server mode — the phone’s speech service (Google or Apple). Only to run the features above. Comments and approved photos are visible to everyone because you sent them for that purpose.

Security

Everything the app sends is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Retention and deletion

Comments and photos are kept until they are deleted. To have anything you sent deleted, email us with the name, text or date so we can find it, or use “Report” in the app. Uninstalling the app removes all data stored on the phone.

Changes

If the app starts sending anything new from your phone, this page is updated and the date above changes.
