Simple version

How to use the lab

Investigate the shape of the Earth through what the night sky actually does. Every number on screen is calculated live from star catalogue positions and the Earth's rotation.

Controls

  • Globes & 3D models: drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • Sky views: drag to look around, scroll or pinch to change the field of view. Hover to read altitude and azimuth.
  • Keyboard: 1–7 switch sections, Space pauses, arrow keys look around a focused sky view, ? opens this guide.
  • Clock: the bar at the bottom of each view controls simulated time – pause, step an hour, change speed, or set any date (UTC).
  • Items with an i or a dotted label show an explanation on hover or tap.

Sections

  1. Explore the Earth – choose where you stand.
  2. Polaris – latitude from the North Star.
  3. Southern Cross – the southern sky's signpost.
  4. Star Trails – long exposures of a turning planet.
  5. Zenith & Horizon – your local frame of reference.
  6. Model Comparison – test a flat-Earth model against observations.
  7. Scientific Evidence – the summary, sources and approximations.

Sky views ignore daylight so that every star above the horizon is visible. Altitudes are geometric (no atmospheric refraction).
